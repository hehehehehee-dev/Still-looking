# Still Looking – Video Script

## Take 1

This is Still Looking.

It helps families of long-missing children picture how their child may look today.

Privately, and honestly.

When a child has been missing for years, the only photo a family has may show a face that no longer exists.

## Take 2

For this demo, I use an AI-generated photo of a child who doesn't exist.

I upload the photo and frame the face.

I add the date of birth, and when the photo was taken.

The app works out the age today.

An AI can suggest marks, like moles or scars.

The family checks them.

It never guesses race.

Now I click Create.

## Take 3

The app shows three possible faces, not one answer.

Each image says which AI model made it.

The confidence note uses real test numbers.

And it always says: share the original photo too.

Photos are never saved.

## Take 4

Most AI apps never test themselves. I did.

I tested on forty-two real people, with photos at different ages.

The honest result: no AI image matched better than the original photo.

Zero out of forty-two.

The AI makes faces look too perfect.

Researchers call this the age-identity trade-off.

## Take 5

So I tested seven ideas, one change at a time.

Most did not help.

But one model, Nano Banana 2, was clearly better.

For children missing over ten years, it found the right person forty-three percent of the time, instead of fourteen percent.

So the app now uses it, with a free backup.

## Take 6

I also found mistakes in my own test, like wrong labels, and fixed them.

Everything, including what failed, is in my learning log.

I built this with help from an AI coding assistant.

Still Looking never says, "this is your child."

It helps families picture their child, and tells them the truth.

Thank you.
